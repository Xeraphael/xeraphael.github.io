abstract: >-
  We present LabVLA, a vision-language-action model for executing scientific laboratory protocols, and RoboGenesis, a simulation-based workflow and data engine for generating demonstrations across robot embodiments. LabVLA combines action-token pretraining with flow-matching posttraining and achieves the highest average success rate among evaluated baselines on LabUtopia in both in-distribution and out-of-distribution settings.
